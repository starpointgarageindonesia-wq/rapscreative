What guests can do with a digital invitation

A digital invitation is a shareable website rather than a printed card. You can send its link through messaging applications so guests can open the event information on their phones. Depending on the selected package, an invitation may include the date and venue, a photo gallery, location directions, a countdown, a guestbook and RSVP responses.

The sample displayed above illustrates a digital wedding invitation for Dimas and Dinda. Its appearance is a visual concept, while the features delivered to a customer are determined by the selected package and the agreed design brief.

Essential and Signature

Essential includes up to five photos, music, countdown, Maps and digital gifting information, with three months of activity. Signature adds more photos, a couple story, guest names, RSVP and a guestbook, with six months of activity. Check which guest features matter before choosing.

Premium and Custom

Premium supports a larger photo collection, a video, an event calendar and personal links, with twelve months of activity. Custom builds on the Premium features with more specific visual direction, two revisions and domain connection. Discuss your event type, images, guest needs and preferred appearance before starting.
